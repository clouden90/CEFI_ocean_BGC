# §2 production loop — profiling provenance & derivations

Every number in the §2 commit / PR traces to an archived artifact here, with its derivation.
**Determinism note:** copies/bytes/syncs/grid/regs/occupancy/SM-SOL are deterministic (set by the
code) and reproduce exactly; transfer *time*, wall-clock, and section timers are run-to-run variable
(~±10%) on the shared node — values are from the run of record.

1 coupling step = **2 COBALT calls**, so nsys per-step totals are divided by 2 for per-call figures.

bit-faithful, not merely within-band.
